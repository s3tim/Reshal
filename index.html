A note from the developer

Why I built Reshal

The terminal is the most powerful tool a developer has. But it's also rigid. You have to remember exact syntax, paths, and flags. I wanted to change that.

Reshal is an experiment: what if the terminal understood you? What if you could just say "find all python files and replace TODO with DONE" and it just worked?

This is an open-source project. No hidden subscriptions, no cloud lock-in. Just a tool that respects your privacy and your time. If it saves you even 10 minutes a day, consider supporting its development.
